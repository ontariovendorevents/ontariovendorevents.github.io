Good to know

What is Ontario Vendor Events?

A free, hand-curated calendar of vendor markets, craft shows and fairs across Ontario. Every listing comes from an organizer's own post or page — nothing is scraped in bulk, and every event is checked before it goes up.

What does each listing show?

The date, time and location, the vendor fee when the organizer publishes one, application deadlines, and how to apply — with a link back to the original post so you can confirm the details yourself.

Who is it for, and what does it cost?

Shoppers looking for markets to visit, and vendors looking for shows to sell at. Browsing is free for everyone, and organizers can submit an event for free.

What are the Vendor, Shopper and Organizer views?

Three ways to read the same calendar, switched at the top of the filters. Vendor view shows fees and how to apply; Shopper view hides the vendor details and leads with times, places and admission; Organizer view adds the listing tools for the people running the markets. The page remembers your pick on this device.

How often is it updated?

The page refreshes from the calendar every morning, and new vendor calls are added most days as organizers post them.

I'm an organizer — how do I get my event listed?

Use the event submission form — it's free, and every submission is reviewed before it appears.

Is there an app?

An Android app is on its way. Until it launches, everything lives here on the page and in the Ontario Vendor Events Facebook community.
